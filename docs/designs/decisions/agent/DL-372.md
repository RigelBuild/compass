decision: "Reload barrier-lift: after `s.stream.Stop()` succeeds and before `h.link.StartAgent`, `reloadLocked` displaces the old Control subscription; after StartAgent returns and the stream swap completes, it sends `AgentControl{replay_complete}` on the replacement served listener. This extends DL-189's fresh-start lift to `Reload` and `RefreshConfig`'s `refreshOneContainer` leg (per DL-081). The runner-local takeover advances the subscription generation, closes its wake channel, and clears liveness without changing retention or the ack cursor. The old stream ends cleanly. A replacement may subscribe before StartAgent returns; because takeover already ran, it remains live and drains the lift. This applies to every backend and does not stop a survivor's in-progress turn. The original lift cannot be re-delivered because `serve` starts from the ack cursor and `AckControl` prunes at-or-below-cursor ops. Sent via `SendControl`/`Send`, never `SendIfLive`"
