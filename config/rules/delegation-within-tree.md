# Delegate within the management tree

Report to your current parent and delegate only within your authorized child
scope; do not route work sideways or skip tree levels. A subagent receives one
self-contained slice with exact targets, contract, acceptance, and non-goals.
Keep parallel file ownership separate, then review the returned diff yourself.

A hands subagent executes its brief, reports results to its caller, and yields;
it does not delegate beyond the brief or claim lane ownership. A Manager
coordinates its assigned lane and remains accountable for delegated results.
Use `skill://management-trees` for the tree and task mechanics.
